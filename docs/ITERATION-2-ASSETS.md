## Final background prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: luxury Aeria website atmospheric background, wide landscape 1536x1024. Create a dreamlike ethereal sky garden, fine-art editorial photography meets exquisite volumetric rendering. Immense soft luminous white cumulus clouds drifting in a pale powder-blue sky, gentle warm sunlight and pearlescent haze, subtle blue-lavender shadows. On the far right, a graceful thin branch covered in delicate white apple blossoms with barely blush-pink centres emerges from cloud mist; a few blossoms and tiny petals drifting near the bottom right. Clouds and blossoms must be exquisite, realistic, soft, alive, airy, richly dimensional. Composition: spacious and minimal left 55 percent, clear luminous near-white sky for dark text; on right 45 percent, a poetic vertical garden of clouds, a delicate flowering branch and sunlit petals, fading into white at bottom. Colour palette: cloud white, washed sky blue, tiny blush-pink accents and faint lilac. No butterfly in this background: an independently animated butterfly will be layered in the website. No text, no logos, no UI, no cards, no buildings, no mountains, no people, no surreal giant flower balls, no neon, no dark tones. Feels like a quiet breath above the clouds, a blossoming new world; refined and emotional rather than a shiny CGI technology object.

## Final butterfly prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent-background butterfly cutout for Aeria premium website, isolated subject. One beautiful anatomically believable delicate pearl-white butterfly in graceful three-quarter flight, facing diagonally upward to the left, the upper wings elegantly raised, visibly separated feather-light forewings and scalloped hindwings. Natural living butterfly, an ethereal lightly translucent wing membrane with very subtle pale-sky-blue iridescence, fine silvery grey delicate veins and the faintest blush near the body. Slender realistic body, fine antennae and extremely elegant silhouette. It should feel alive, soft, weightless and sophisticated, like a sunlit white butterfly in a cloud garden. Soft diffuse daylight, fine-art macro photography, high detail and clean silhouette. Whole butterfly completely visible, centred with generous margin. No flowers or branches, no background or clouds, genuinely transparent alpha background. No glass sculpture, no chrome, no plastic, no jewel encrustation, no harsh black outline, no oversized insect body, no neon colours, no other butterflies, no text.

## Final foreground cloud prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent foreground cloud layer for an ethereal website, very wide landscape 1536x640. A single soft billowing bank of magnificent sunlit white cumulus clouds across the bottom edge of the image, rising irregularly to about 60 percent of image height. Delicate wispy edges, realistic fine volumetric texture, subtle mist-blue and faint lavender in the shadows, luminous diffuse daylight. Pure white at the very bottom of the cloud bank, with the top half and all the air around the cloud silhouettes genuinely transparent, alpha cutout. No sky colour filling the transparent upper half. Elegant, airy, feather-light, cinematic high-quality cloud photography. Cloud volume distributed asymmetrically, higher on the right, a little lower left, like flying above a cloud sea. No butterflies, no flowers, no text, no objects, no hard edges, no dark storm clouds.
